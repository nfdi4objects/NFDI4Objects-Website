# Learning Materials: The FAIR OER of NFDI4Objects

## FAIR OER

The NFDI4Objects OER (Open Educational Resources) are learning modules covering topics addressed by the consortium. They are characterised by their free accessibility, clear licensing, and the possibility of reusing them in one’s own teaching activities and adapting them to individual needs. (For a definition of OER, see also [UNESCO 2019](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000383205).) The FAIR OER of NFDI4Objects therefore serve both as self-study resources and as teaching materials.

Our offering includes introductory materials, guides, tutorials, workflows, exercises, slide decks, workshop materials, and even complete courses combining different OER components.

The materials are developed and made available in collaboration with the NFDI4Objects Qualification Service.

They are based on the [FAIR-OER-Templates](https://nfdi4objects.github.io/oer-template-dokumentation/) also provided by NFDI4Objects.
