The next online meeting of the “Protected Heritage Sites” Community Cluster will take place on **Tuesday, April 28, from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.**

The meeting will focus on finalizing the data models for intervention sites and protected areas. This is expected to be the last meeting in which these models will be discussed in detail.

If you are unable to attend, you have the option to submit your comments in advance via email so that they can be included in the discussion.

**Zoom:** [Link](https://zoom.us/j/91094521138?pwd=zJLwXZetdBcpMpssA9RpZPVCRFUWDP.1)

The transcript of the March 12 meeting is already available via OSF:[https://osf.io/pwzg4/](https://osf.io/pwzg4/)


You will also find the current drafts of the data models there.
