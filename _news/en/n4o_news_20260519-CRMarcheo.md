The June edition of the “KultDig” lunch will also be held in collaboration with the CAA on May 29, 2026, from 12:00 to 1:30 p.m.

This supplementary webcast will focus on CRMarchaeo, an extension of CIDOC CRM developed specifically for archaeological excavation processes and related entities and activities.

Together with Stephen Stead, one of the founding members of the CIDOC CRM Special Interest Group (CRM-SIG), we will examine international standards and scientific principles for modeling stratigraphy.

Registration is open until May 27: [https://event-kulturspeicher.schleswig-holstein.de/#/events/bd85b3ca-c200-408a-b118-c5dd5b9bcac1](https://event-kulturspeicher.schleswig-holstein.de/#/ events/bd85b3ca-c200-408a-b118-c5dd5b9bcac1)

We look forward to your participation!

No prior knowledge or participation in the February webcast is required. The presentation will be held in English and is free of charge.
