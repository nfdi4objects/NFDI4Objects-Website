This winter term, the Digital Classicist Seminar will once again take place every fortnight on Tuesdays, from 4.00 pm sharp, at the Centre for Basic Research on the Ancient World of the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities, Unter den Linden 8, Room 07W04, Berlin-Mitte.

In the 2026/27 academic year, the focus will be on ‘Measures, Models and Patterns in Classical Studies’.

Our Task Area 2 is represented by contributions from Mattia Celisi and Karsten Tolle, who are exploring the application of artificial intelligence in numismatic research and data processing.

Click here for the programme: [https://digital-classicist.bbaw.de/programme/programm-2026/27#c104](https://digital-classicist.bbaw.de/programme/programm-2026/27#c104)
