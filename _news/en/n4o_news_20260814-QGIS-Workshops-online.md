Since 2025, the **DASV e.V. Digitalization Working Group** has been organizing a workshop series on QGIS in cooperation with **NFDI4Objects**. The 90-minute workshops are aimed at beginners — no prior knowledge is required. The events were recorded and are **available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DigitalisierungDASV).**

More information about the individual workshops and the recordings can be found in the **[workshop series event](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/events/n4o_activity_20250715-1837/)** on the NFDI4Objects website.
