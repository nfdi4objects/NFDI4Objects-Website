The program for the WissKI User Meeting on October 20–21 is available online at: [https://wiss-ki.eu/wat26-agenda](https://wiss-ki.eu/wat26-agenda)!

The WissKI community will meet again this year **on October 20, 2026 from 10:00 to 17:00 and October 21, 2026 from 9:30 to 15:15 in the Aufseß Hall of the Germanic National Museum in Nuremberg**, to discuss projects and topics, to stay informed about current developments and to develop joint solutions to challenges. Most of the event will take place in german, however some Presentations will be held in english.
