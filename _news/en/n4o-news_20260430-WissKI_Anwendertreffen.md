Dear WissKI community and everyone interested in Linked Open Data Management,

Once again this year, we cordially invite all WissKI users and developers to join us to discuss projects and topics, learn about current developments, and work together to develop solutions to challenges. 

The **8th WissKI User Meeting** will take place:

- **October 20, 2026:** 10:00 AM–5:00 PM
- **October 21, 2026:** 9:00 AM–3:00 PM
- **Location:** [Aufseß Hall** at the Germanisches Nationalmuseum in Nuremberg](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Qs61aCbPD51yEWbk8)

Contributions in the form of presentations and workshops can be submitted until **August 31, 2026**. Your submissions will be published in a **Book of Abstracts** this year! Learn more in our [“Call for Abstracts”](https://wiss-ki.eu/sites/default/files/wat26/WissKI_Anwender_innentreffen_2026-Call_for_Abstracts.pdf).

You can already view a [preliminary program schedule](https://wiss-ki.eu/wat26-programm) online!

[Registration](https://wiss-ki.eu/wat26-anmeldung) to participate in the WissKI User Meeting is open through **September 30, 2026**.

If you have any questions about the WissKI User Meeting, please email [info@wiss-ki.eu](mailto:info@wiss-ki.eu) or message us on our [Mattermost WAT Channel](https://chat.wiss-ki.eu/wisski/channels/wat).


Best regards,

The organizers of #WAT26
