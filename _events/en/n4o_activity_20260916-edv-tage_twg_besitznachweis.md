Under the title **‘Museums and Archives in Times of Crisis: Preserving Cultural Heritage – Securing Data’**, this year’s EDV-Tage will focus on how museums and archives can remain operational even under uncertain conditions and in times of cyber-attacks, as well as how they can safeguard their (digital) cultural heritage in sustainable ways.

NFDI4Objects is contributing a presentation from the [TWG Proof of Ownership](/_twgs/de/besitznachweis-im-verlustfall-use-case-für-den-minimaldatensatz.md) to the panel **“Digitisation as a Safeguarding Strategy”**.

The full programme and details on how to participate online are available on the organiser’s website: [www.edvtage.de](https://www.edvtage.de).
