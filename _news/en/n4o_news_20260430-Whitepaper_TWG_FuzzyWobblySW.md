#### The NFDI4Objects TWG [Community Standards for Modeling Fuzziness & Wobbliness in Research Data Using Semantic Web Technologies and Formalisms (FuzzyWobblySW)](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/link?external_url=https%3A%2F%2Fzenodo.org%2Frecords%2F14893827) has published a new white paper that defines the terms uncertainty, vagueness, and “fuzziness & wobbliness” from the TWG’s perspective. 

The document compares and summarizes working definitions developed collaboratively by the FuzzyWobblySW TWG. The goal is to establish a common conceptual and modeling framework. By clarifying the concepts and presenting modeling strategies (e.g., probabilistic approaches, gradual semantic mappings, Wikibase implementations), the work contributes to more transparent, interoperable, and FAIR-compliant solutions for uncertain, imprecise, or ambiguous information in cultural heritage knowledge graphs.

**The [white paper](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/assemblies/commons/f/1/proposals/90) is open for comments as part of the NFDI4Objects Commons process until May 28, 2026!**
