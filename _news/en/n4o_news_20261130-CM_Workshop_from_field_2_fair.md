Dear colleagues,
Metadata is the key to ensuring that datasets remain usable, discoverable and reusable in the long term. We therefore invite you to the online workshop **‘From the Field to FAIR: Approaches to Minimal Metadata for the Interoperability of Near-Surface Geophysical Data’** on Monday 30 November 2026 from 9 am to 11 am.

The workshop is divided into two parts. The first part offers an insight into the current state of development of the metadata model [https://zenodo.org/records/20138538](https://zenodo.org/records/20138538). The second part is practice-oriented: using test data and a template derived from the ontology, participants will work together to record metadata. The focus will be on hands-on experimentation, sharing experiences, and discussing use cases and challenges.

The link to the workshop can be obtained by emailing Johannes Rabiger-Völlmer:
[johannes.rabiger-voellmer@ufz.de](johannes.rabiger-voellmer@ufz.de)

The workshop is taking place as part of an inter-consortium use case involving the NFDI consortia FAIRagro and NFDI4Objects.

We look forward to a lively turnout.
