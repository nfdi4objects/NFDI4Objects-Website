This analysis examines the current situation regarding research data and infrastructure in museum and university collections. It aims to identify the current state of affairs and existing practices relating to the research, cataloguing and digitisation of these collections. It also highlights widely used standards, vocabularies and tools, as well as the challenges involved in working with collection-related research data.
