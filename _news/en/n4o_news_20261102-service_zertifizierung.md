**The Service Monitoring Team is pleased to announce: You can now submit applications for the certification of technical services for the NFDI4Objects service portfolio at [https://community.nfdi4objects.net/de/assemblies/SMT/f/54/surveys/3](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/de/assemblies/SMT/f/54/surveys/3).**
Please note that only applications for services that are in full release or live operation can be processed.

### When can you submit your application?
Applications can be submitted at any time from now on. Meetings of the Certification Board take place 2–3 times a year, depending on the volume of applications. The next meeting will take place in January. If you wish your service to be considered at this meeting, a complete application must be submitted by 6 December 2026.

### Where can you find further information?

Document setting out the certification criteria used to assess services: [https://community.nfdi4objects.net/de/assemblies/commons/f/2/proposals/100](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/de/assemblies/commons/f/2/proposals/100)

Full questionnaire for the certification application: [https://www.nfdi4objects.net/Zertifizierungsantr%C3%A4ge-Technische-Dienste-Full-Release.pdf] (https://www.nfdi4objects.net/Zertifizierungsantr%C3%A4ge-Technische-Dienste-Full-Release.pdf)

### Do you have any questions?
If you have any questions about the certification process, please use our contact form on the NFDI4Objects Helpdesk at [https://www.nfdi4objects.net/help/](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/help/) and select the ‘Service Certification’ category.
