## Description

[ ] Description @ EN

To improve the discoverability and usability of cultural heritage data generated by state heritage agencies, universities, and citizen scientists, among others, NFDI4Objects is developing several INSPIRE- and OGC-compliant online geodata services. Based on [exchange interfaces](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/services/schnittstellen-zum-austausch-von-denkmal-und-restaurierungsbezogenen-daten/), these services will provide data on cultural heritage objects, measure objects, and protected sites, and will also depict legal implications, such as those arising from the various state monument protection laws. This will enable a daily updated view of the data in the archives and systems of the data holders. To safeguard the protection interests of the data-holding parties, data access is regulated via a tiered access rights system. The geodata services can be integrated into users’ own information systems via interfaces. This enables cross-system data exchange.
