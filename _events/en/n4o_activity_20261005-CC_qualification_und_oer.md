After a long summer break, the Qualification & Open Educational Resources (OER) Community Cluster is meeting again. The meeting is an open forum for the whole community: come and join the discussion, share your experiences and help us develop our ideas further.

### The ‘Cookbook’ needs your perspective

Our “Cookbook” is still just an idea at this stage, which is precisely why now is the best time to get involved. It is intended to form the basis for a white paper on a distributed OER infrastructure in NFDI4Objects. What do you think is essential to include? Which “recipes” would you like to help develop? We look forward to your ideas and contributions!

### Presenting your own OER

Are you interested in presenting your own Open Educational Resources as part of the Community Cluster? If so, please get in touch with Gerda Grimm (@GerdaGrimm on the Community Hub) or by email at gerda.grimm@dainst.de.
