We would like to draw your attention to the following job vacancy at our co-applicant, the Klassik Stiftung Weimar:

The Goethe and Schiller Archive, Digital Editions Department, is seeking a research assistant (m/f/d) specialising in Digital Humanities. The post is available immediately, is on a fixed-term contract until 31 December 2028 and is remunerated in accordance with pay scale E 13 of the TV-L.

As part of the academic consortium project [‘PROPYLÄEN. Research Platform on Goethe’s Biographica’](https://www.klassik-stiftung.de/goethe-und-schiller-archiv/projekte/propylaeen/), the role involves further developing and coordinating digital editorial work processes, methods and data models. This includes advising the editorial teams, ensuring the quality of research data and evaluating AI-supported methods, for example for the transcription and annotation of historical sources.

Candidates are expected to hold a university degree (in the humanities, digital humanities, computer science or similar), have experience in digital editorial work, very good knowledge of XML/TEI and good programming skills (e.g. Python or JavaScript), as well as very good German and good English. We offer flexible working hours, the option to work remotely, a company pension scheme (VBL), opportunities for further training and free admission to the Foundation’s museums.
