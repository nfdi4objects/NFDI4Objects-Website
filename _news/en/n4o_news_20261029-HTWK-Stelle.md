The research project “V.E.R.B. – Lost Heritage – Reconstructed Buildings. Built Heritage as a Resource for Social Resilience” is seeking a Research Assistant (m/f/d) in the field of computer vision and digital cultural heritage (100%) with the opportunity to pursue a PhD.

This joint project between HTWK Leipzig and the Leibniz Institute for Spatial Social Research (IRS) investigates the interplay between social memory, digital technologies and changes to the built environment.

The sub-project at HTWK Leipzig focuses on AI-supported analysis of historical image collections, the spatial localisation of historical photographs and the digital 3D reconstruction of lost buildings.

So, if you know anyone who is interested in machine learning, computer vision and digital cultural heritage, please feel free to apply by 10 November 2026!
