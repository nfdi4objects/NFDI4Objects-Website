The cluster co-chairs, Nina Hohm and Sandra Schröer-Spang, cordially invite you to the **second meeting of the NFDI4Objects Community Cluster on FDM Consulting**.

The cluster is aimed at anyone involved in providing FDM consulting services to researchers in the field of material cultural heritage who would like to discuss practical consulting needs and the consortium’s offerings with representatives from NFDI4Objects.

**When: June 29, 2026, 10:00–11:00 a.m.**
**Where: online**

In our second meeting, [**Dr. Patrick Helling**](https://dch.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/ueber-das-dch/team/dr-patrick-helling) (coordinator at the Data Center for the Humanities at the University of Cologne) will provide insight into his **[dissertation](https://doi.org/10.18716/dch/c.00000002), published in 2025**, with the talk:
*“Ein neuer Ansatz zur Definition von FDM-Bedarfen - Entwicklung eines formalen Beschreibungsmodells für das Management von digitalen Forschungsdaten”.*

Afterward, we’ll discuss **practical consulting needs** and **news from NFDI4Objects**.

Do you already have **topics you’d like to see covered**, current **needs**, or **questions for NFDI4Objects** that we can discuss during the session in addition to the main topic? Then **just write to us directly** (sandra.schroeer@hs-mainz.de or n.hohm@ub.uni-frankfurt.de) or leave a **[comment on the NFDI4Objects Community Hub](https://community. nfdi4objects.net/assemblies/FDM-Beratung/f/19/meetings/37#comments)** (for registered users).

We look forward to a lively turnout!
