The CAA DE Workshop 2026 will take place this year **from September 17 to 18 in conjunction with the CAA NL/FL**. The workshop is organized by Jan Miera from the Department of Prehistoric and Early History Archaeology at the History Department of the University of Münster.

As in previous years, the workshop offers archaeologists, computer scientists, and especially young researchers and students a platform to present and discuss their ongoing work and findings, build networks, and discover new opportunities for collaboration.

The **Call for Papers** is **now open** and [available](https://www.uni-muenster.de/UrFruehGeschichte/CAA2026/cfp.html). The conference language is English, but German presentations are also welcome.

Please send your abstracts and presentation proposals (max. 250 words) **by May 15, 2026, directly to the organizer.**

Email Jan Miera: [jmiera@uni-muenster.de](jmiera@uni-muenster.de)

Further information and details can be found on the [workshop website](https://ag-caa.de/).
