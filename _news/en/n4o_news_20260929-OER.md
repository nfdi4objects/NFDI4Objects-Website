Using the [Quarto templates for creating FAIR Open Educational Resources (OER)](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/services/nfdi4objects-qualification/# included in the offering), existing teaching materials can be quickly and easily converted into various formats and published on different systems without the need for time-consuming adjustments each time. The templates also help to ensure that teaching content is made available in an open, transparent and reusable manner.

[Quarto](https://quarto.org/) is an open-source publishing system that allows you to create high-quality documents and web pages from simple text and media files. The content is managed centrally, regardless of how it will ultimately be displayed, and can be exported in various formats (such as PDF or as an HTML web page). It is not tied to any specific publishing platform.

The NFDI4Objects templates take a great deal of the work off your hands: **the structure, design and a metadata schema are already in place** and can be adapted to your own requirements. You can then concentrate on your content.

At the same time, the templates support the FAIR principles and thus ensure transparent publication and improved reusability of your OER.

Detailed [step-by-step documentation](https://nfdi4objects.github.io/oer-template-dokumentation/) makes it easy to get started. 

**Feel free to give the templates a go!**
