#### The NFDI4Culture Art History Award recognises data publications and academic works that address topics in art and cultural history and, in doing so, make a significant contribution to the objectives within the consortium’s areas of responsibility.  

#### From among all applications, the jury from the art history community within NFDI4Culture will select up to five entries to receive awards in two categories.  
  
Call for entries: NFDI4Culture Art History Award  
  
#### Submission period: 1 July – 30 September 2026  
  
#### Further information: [https://nfdi4culture.de/de/nachrichten/details/nfdi4culture-art-history-award-aha.html](https://nfdi4culture.de/de/nachrichten/details/nfdi4culture-art-history-award-aha.html)
