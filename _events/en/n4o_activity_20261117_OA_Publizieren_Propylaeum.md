#### The information session organised by [Propylaeum – Specialist Information Service for Classical Studies](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/services/propylaeum/) provides a concise overview of the basics of academic publishing in Open Access. Drawing on the publication options offered by Propylaeum E-Publishing and the services provided by NFDI4Culture and NFDI4Objects, the session will provide practical answers to key questions such as the following:

- What options do I have, as an author, to publish my article, thesis or monograph in Open Access?
- What options are available to editors for publishing conference proceedings or launching an academic e-journal?
- Is a printed edition possible alongside an open-access publication?  
- What impact does open access have on visibility, reputation and academic recognition?
- What funding opportunities are available for open-access publications in classical studies?
- What exactly are ORCID IDs and what advantages do they offer researchers?  
- How can research data generated in connection with a publication be published and documented in a sustainable manner?
- What specific issues arise regarding image rights and licences? Under what conditions can copyright-protected images be published, and what advantages can Open Access offer when clarifying usage rights?

#### The event is aimed at students and academics in the field of classical studies at all stages of their careers.
