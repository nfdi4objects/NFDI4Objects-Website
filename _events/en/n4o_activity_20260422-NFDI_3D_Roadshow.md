### [NFDI4Ing](https://nfdi4ing.de/), [NFDI4Culture](https://nfdi4culture.de/index.html) and [NFDI4Objects](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/) will be presenting their activities and results to date in the field of 3D **on April 22 from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.** ###

- for the fields of archaeology, architecture, and civil engineering, as well as cultural heritage objects and collections
- including the provided workflows, tools, services, and guidelines
- covering topics such as data collection, storage, and publication in repositories; options for long-term archiving; standards such as IIIF for dissemination and PIDs for identification and discoverability; presentation in viewers; appropriate vocabularies; and examples in use cases

### Our Speakers ###

We will hear insights from **Jakob Beetz and Roger Winkler** (NFDI4Ing), **Zoe Schubert and Katja Sternitzke** (NFDI4Culture), and **Matthias Lang and Valentina Salcedo-Paparoni** (NFDI4Objects).

Following the presentations, there will be time for an open discussion with the speakers. 

### Organizational Information ###

The event is free of charge. The presentations will be held in German. Questions in English are welcome.
