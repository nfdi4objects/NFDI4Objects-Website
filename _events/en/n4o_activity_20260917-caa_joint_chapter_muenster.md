<strong>On 17 and 18 September</strong>, the joint conference of the [CAA Netherlands/Flanders](https://nlfl.caa-international.org/) and the [CAA Germany](https://ag-caa.de/) will take place at the University of Münster.

NDFI4Objects will be represented with various contributions from Task Areas 1, 2, 4 and 6 in the thematic area of **Linked Open Data, the Semantic Web and AI-supported workflows**.

Full details of the event and the programme can be found on the organiser’s website: [https://www.uni-muenster.de/UrFruehGeschichte/caa2026.shtml](https://www.uni-muenster.de/UrFruehGeschichte/caa2026.shtml).
