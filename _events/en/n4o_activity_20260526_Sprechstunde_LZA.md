NFDI4Objects has recently started offering regular consultation hours on long-term archiving. These take place on the last Tuesday of every month and can also be scheduled individually as needed.

The consultation hours are open to everyone; topics for discussion include general questions about file formats, documentation, and potential automation solutions, as well as IANUS’s long-term archiving services!  For detailed questions regarding data or its organization, please send a brief email in advance to [ianus-fdz@dainst.de](mailto:ianus-fdz@dainst.de).

**Date: May 26, 2026 (every last Tuesday of the month)**

**Time: 10:00 a.m.**

**Zoom access details:** [https://meet.dainst.org/rooms/dmj-don-u0q-yao/join](https://meet.dainst.org/rooms/dmj-don-u0q-yao/join)

We look forward to the discussion!
