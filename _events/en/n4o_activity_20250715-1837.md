Since 2025, the **Digitalization Working Group of the [Dachverband Archäologischer Studierendenvertretungen e.V. (DASV e.V.)](https://www.dasv-ev.org/)** has been organizing a workshop series on QGIS for archaeologists.

Since the [2nd Workshop](#2-qgis-workshop-july-2025), the series has been held in cooperation with **NFDI4Objects**, with **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/) as instructor**.

The slides and datasets from previous workshops have been published on Zenodo and can be accessed via the following link:

**[Link to the workshop slides on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18347010)**

## Current Workshop
### 4th QGIS Workshop

The 4th QGIS Workshop organized by the **Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V.** will take place **online on July 16, 2026, at 6:00 PM (s.t.)**. The instructor will be **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/)** from **NFDI4Objects**.

This session will focus on **digitizing and mapping in QGIS**.

**Additional topic requests** can be submitted until **July 10** using the following form: **[Link to the form](https://forms.gle/v9E2jkxvNZMo2NmH7)**

You can join the event via the following link:  
**[Zoom link](https://uni-hamburg.zoom.us/j/6486455347?pwd=soCEP79NOZVqJy4P53svE03Ng6rsa7.1&omn=63157524821)**

### 3rd QGIS Workshop – January 2026

The 3rd QGIS Workshop organized by the **Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V.** took place online via Zoom on **January 22, 2026, at 6:00 PM (s.t.)** in cooperation with **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/)** from **NFDI4Objects**.

### 2nd QGIS Workshop – July 2025

The 2nd QGIS Workshop organized by the **Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V.** took place online via Zoom on **July 24, 2025, at 6:00 PM (s.t.)** in cooperation with **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/)** from **NFDI4Objects**.

The workshop introduced the fundamentals of **geodata, geographic information systems, and Quantum GIS**. Participants were also able to submit additional **topic requests** in advance via a form.

### 1st QGIS Workshop – January 2025

The first virtual QGIS Workshop organized by the Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V. took place online via Zoom on **January 31, 2025** and was organized by two students.

The workshop covered a **basic introduction to QGIS** as well as the **development of a first small project**.
