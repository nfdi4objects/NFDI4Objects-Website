The online workshop **‘From the Field to FAIR: Approaches to Minimal Metadata for the Interoperability of Near-Surface Geophysical Data’** will take place as part of the CC Geophysical Prospecting on Monday 30 November 2026 from 9 am to 11 am.

The ontology, based on the annotated ‘Minimal Metadata Terminology’, has reached an initial final stage. In addition to the method-independent metadata, metadata for magnetics, FDEM and gamma spectroscopy have also been integrated. We would like to present this progress to you during the 4th meeting, which we will be organising as an online workshop.

During the workshop, we will provide you with:

(1) an insight into the current state of development of the metadata model ([https://zenodo.org/records/20138538](https://zenodo.org/records/20138538)) and
(2) the opportunity to try out the metadata model in practice and to discuss experiences, potential use cases and existing challenges together.

The workshop is taking place as part of an inter-consortium use case involving the NFDI consortia FAIRagro and NFDI4Objects.

If you are interested, please contact us directly for registration details (johannes.rabiger-voellmer@ufz.de).

Further information: [https://community.nfdi4objects.net/en/assemblies/GP/f/36/meetings/45](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/en/assemblies/GP/f/36/meetings/45)
