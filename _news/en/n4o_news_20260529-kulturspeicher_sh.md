On 29 May 2026, from 12:00 to 13:30, there will be an introduction to [CRMarchaeo](https://cidoc-crm.org/crmarchaeo).
In February, at the KultDig Lunch, we were introduced to CIDOC CRM – an object-oriented, conceptual reference model and, at the same time, a standard ontology for cultural heritage.  
By popular demand, we are now taking it a step further: this follow-up webcast focuses on CRMarchaeo, an extension of CIDOC CRM developed specifically for archaeological excavation processes and the associated entities and activities.  
Together with Stephen Stead, one of the founding members of the CIDOC CRM Special Interest Group (CRM-SIG), we will explore how international standards and scientific principles are rethinking the modelling of stratigraphy.  
We very much look forward to your participation!  
No prior knowledge or attendance at the February webcast is required. The presentation will be held in English.
