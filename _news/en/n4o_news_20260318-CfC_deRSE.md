The **de-RSE Collaboration Workshop 2026** is currently seeking submissions for interactive sessions, lightning talks, and collaborative formats centered on **research software engineering.** The workshop focuses on working together, problem-solving, and tangible results—ideas that often emerge only during coffee breaks are meant to be put into practice here.

Topics range from software craftsmanship, education, and open science to AI (including the special workshop “AI-supported Research Software Engineering”), community building, policy, and career development. Formats such as fishbowl sessions, hackathons, open discussion panels, or tutorials are welcome.

**Submissions are accepted until April 15, 2026 (AOE).** Further details and the submission form are available on GitHub: [Link](https://github.com/DE-RSE/2026_collaboration_workshop_de).
