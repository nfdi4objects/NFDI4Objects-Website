You are cordially invited to a cross-consortium discussion on digital provenance research on Thursday, 21 May 2026, 1.00 pm to 2.00 pm via Webex: [https://spk-berlin.webex.com/meet/a.berthold](https://spk-berlin.webex.com/meet/a.berthold).  

We will be exploring the activities relating to digital provenance research within the NFDI consortia [4Culture](https://nfdi4culture.de/), [4Memory](https://4memory.de/) and 4Objects, and would like to discuss these with you. Colleagues from the 4Culture and 4Memory consortia will provide a brief overview of the resources available on this topic and will be on hand to answer questions and engage in discussion.  

No registration is required; we look forward to exchanging ideas with you!
