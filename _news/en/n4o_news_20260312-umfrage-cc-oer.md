As part of the NFDI4Objects community cluster “Qualification & Open Educational Resources,” a survey is currently being conducted through March 31, 2026.

The aim of the survey is to investigate how widespread the creation of Open Educational Resources (OER) is within the fields of material culture and object studies, and what infrastructures are currently used for the creation, provision, and dissemination of OER.

It consists of 9 questions and takes about 10 minutes to complete.

Click here to take the survey: [Survey](https://survey.nfdi4objects.net/index.php/769299?lang=de)

Thank you in advance for your participation!
