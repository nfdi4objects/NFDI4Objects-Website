## How can vocabularies, authority data and metadata standards for buildings be better linked and made usable for the community’s needs?

At the follow-up meeting, which will focus on ‘vocabularies’, we will discuss the requirements for the description and indexing of buildings together with representatives from various institutions and specialist communities. The focus will be on:

- the user’s perspective
- building authority data and minimum data sets
- interoperability
- opportunities for process automation

In addition to expert input and discussion, there will also be time set aside for a collaborative working session.

### Date: 26 and 27 January 2027 (lunch to lunch)  
### Venue: [Klassik Stiftung Weimar, event room in the palace, Burgplatz 4, 99423 Weimar](https://www.klassik-stiftung.de/en/)

Please make a note of the date for now; further information will follow soon in the [event listing](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/de/events/n4o_activity_20270126-cross_nfdi_bauwerksnormdaten/).
