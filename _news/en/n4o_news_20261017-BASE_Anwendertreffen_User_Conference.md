## 3rd BASE4NFDI User Conference on 24 and 25 November 2026 in Berlin

Registration for this year’s BASE4NFDI User Conference is now open: [https://events.gwdg.de/e/uc4b2026](https://events.gwdg.de/e/uc4b2026).

The conference will take place at the Berlin State Library on Potsdamer Platz from 24 to 25 November 2026. Attendance is free of charge.
