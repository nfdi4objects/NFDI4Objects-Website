A common structure comprising data fields that can be used in different contexts, enabling information to be shared between different, technically independent systems.

Developed by researchers, administrators and users of cultural heritage data through a collaborative process within the ‘Protected Heritage Sites’ CC.

This document is intended for those responsible for the maintenance and management of cultural heritage objects at the data level.
