## Description

In conservation-restoration and heritage management, heterogeneous technical terminology leads to significant problems in metadata management. Only 27.5% of the 240 restorers surveyed use standardized vocabularies, which are mostly internal. A controlled, machine-readable technical vocabulary for archaeological conservation-restoration is completely lacking. While 94.3% of heritage management authorities use controlled vocabularies, these are predominantly internal.

The TRAIL closes these gaps by integrating LEIZA’s “SKOS-ified” technical thesaurus for conservation-restoration and a thesaurus for dating periods into the central vocabulary service DANTE. The web application “conserFAIRy,” which provides subject matter experts with low-threshold access to SKOS modeling, is being further developed. Using the Cocoda software, exemplary mappings to additional vocabularies will be carried out, and methodological questions regarding mapping decisions will be examined.

Within the framework of TRAILS, reusable workflows and best-practice examples from the fields of heritage management and conservation-restoration are being developed, which can serve as blueprints for the entire community. The workflows address the development of FAIR domain-specific vocabularies and their integration into central vocabulary servers, mapping with other controlled vocabularies, and the use of these vocabularies for tagging entities.
