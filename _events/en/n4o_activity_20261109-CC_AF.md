We are delighted to invite you to the NFDI4Objects CC Authority Files and Community-driven Vocabularies cluster meeting **on 9 November 2026, from 1.00 pm to 2.00 pm**.

As part of the [Culture Cloud](https://culturecloud-niedersachsen.de/) project, a standard data editorial team for archives, libraries, heritage conservation and museums is currently being established in Lower Saxony. Through shared pools, this centre will maintain DANTE records for individuals, organisations, (historical) places and subject vocabularies. Co-Chair Michael Markert will present the current considerations at both a technical and organisational level and provide an insight into the opportunities arising from the comprehensive, standardised interlinking of the diverse collections.
