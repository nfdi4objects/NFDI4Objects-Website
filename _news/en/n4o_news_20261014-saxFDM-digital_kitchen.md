The SaxFDM Digital Kitchen for <strong>October</strong> will take place <strong>on Thursday 22 October 2026 from 1.00 pm to 2.00 pm</strong> with Jan Rohden from the DFG. 

**Topic:** [Research Data Management Through the Ages – How References to RDM in DFG Applications Have Evolved](https://saxfdm.de/veranstaltungen/fdm-dfg/)

Research funding organisations are increasingly requiring applicants to provide mandatory details on the handling of research data in their funding applications. However, the specific implications of such requirements for applications remain largely unclear. This presentation examines how research data management (RDM) is addressed in grant applications and how these descriptions have evolved over time and across different disciplines. To answer this question, a comprehensive corpus of applications from the ‘Sachbeihilfe’ funding programme, submitted to the German Research Foundation (DFG) between 2017 and 2025, is analysed. The analysis employs methods from natural language processing (NLP), machine learning and the automated detection of references to repositories.

Whilst the results point to a general increase in awareness of FDM, they also indicate significant differences between the various academic disciplines in terms of how the handling of research data is described. Furthermore, discernible changes in the information provided on research data since the introduction of the relevant DFG checklist in 2021 suggest that guideline-based approaches adopted by funding organisations are having an impact on the information provided on FDM in grant applications. This provides new insights for the monitoring and design of open science strategies.
