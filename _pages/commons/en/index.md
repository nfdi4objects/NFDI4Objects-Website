# Commons: Recommendations and Standards from the Community

The NFDI4Objects Commons represent **suggestions, recommendations, and standards from our community** and document, for example, **best practices, technical specifications, and recommendations for service development**. In this way, the Commons provide continuously updated access to quality-assured resources from our community.

Commons contributions are created in the [Community Clusters](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/community/groups-networks/community-clusters/), [Temporary Working Groups](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/community/groups-networks/twgs/), [TRAILs](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/community/groups-networks/trails/), and [Task Areas](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/areas/). The proposals then go through a participatory and transparent [quality assurance process](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/de/assemblies/commons), during which the drafts are open to public discussion and comment. This enables all experts to contribute directly to the quality assurance of our work products. The public discussion of the contributions takes place on the [NFDI4Objects Community Hub](https://community.nfdi4objects.net/). The Commons can be revised, updated, and further developed at any time if new requirements, technical developments, or professional input make this necessary.
