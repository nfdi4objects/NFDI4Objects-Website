The 6th FORGE Conference will take place from **27 to 29 September 2027 at the Academy of Sciences and Literature Mainz**. Unlike in previous years, FORGE 27 will be jointly organised by a team representing several institutions, together with the four humanities and cultural studies NFDI consortia (Humanities@NFDI).

The conference, organised by the DHd Working Group [Research Data Management in the Humanities (gwFDM)](https://digitalhumanities.de/ag-geisteswissenschaftliches-forschungsdatenmanagement-gwfdm/), brings together stakeholders from academia, data infrastructures and research data management who work on the creation, use, processing, interlinking and long-term preservation of research data in the humanities.

**Further information on the conference theme, the Call for Papers (CfP) and the programme will be published on the conference website [forge27.de](https://forge27.de).**
