Dear colleagues, we would like to draw your attention to the following two events organised by the LIDO Service Station:

On 11 December, from 1 pm to 2 pm, the LIDO Service Station of NFDI4Culture is hosting an information session for all GLAM communities. Just pop in and feel free to ask any questions you may have! More information is available here: [https://nfdi4culture.de/id/E7160](https://nfdi4culture.de/id/E7160)

On 5 February, from 9 am to 10 am, there will be a talk on the topic “LIDO & Linked Art: two sides of the same coin?” Whilst LIDO relies on a strictly hierarchical data model, Linked Art is based on a graph-based model, through which metadata is made available as Linked (Open) Data. The talk promises to shed light on both data models and weigh up their respective advantages and disadvantages. You can find all the relevant information here: [https://nfdi4culture.de/id/E7162](https://nfdi4culture.de/id/E7162)
