This contribution from the NFDI4Objects [TWG – Community Standards for Modelling Fuzziness & Wobbliness in Research Data Using Semantic Web Technologies and Formalisms (FuzzyWobblySW)](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/twgs/community-standards-for-modelling-fuzziness-wobbliness-in-research-data-using-semantic-web-technologies-and-formalisms-fuzzywobblysw/) provides a basic explanation and definition of the terms: uncertainty, vagueness, and ‘fuzziness & wobbliness’ from the TWG’s perspective. These definitions serve as the basis for the preparation of further white papers by the TWG.
