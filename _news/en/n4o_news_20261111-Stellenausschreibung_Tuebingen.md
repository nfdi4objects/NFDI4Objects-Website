To strengthen the research data management team of the Cluster of Excellence for the Integrative Study of Human Origins [(HUMAN ORIGINS)](https://uni-tuebingen.de/de/297444) at the University of Tübingen, which has been running since the start of the year, the project is seeking a Data Scientist (m/f/d, E13 TV-L, 100%, fixed-term until 31 December 2032). The cluster is dedicated to the interdisciplinary study of human evolution over the last five million years by integrating biological, cultural and ecological perspectives.

The ideal candidate will have in-depth knowledge of data science and (research) software engineering to support the cluster’s interdisciplinary research teams by developing bespoke software tools and adapting existing ones in collaboration with the data stewards. The cluster’s FDM team is jointly supported and led by the two core facilities, the Digital Humanities Centre and the Quantitative Biology Centre.

Full details of the duties involved and the required profile can be found in the job advertisement at: [https://uni-tuebingen.de/universitaet/karriere/newsfullview-stellenangebote/article/data-scientist-m-f-d-e13-tv-l-100/](https://uni-tuebingen.de/universitaet/karriere/newsfullview-stellenangebote/article/data-scientist-m-f-d-e13-tv-l-100/)

**The deadline for applications is 15 November!**
