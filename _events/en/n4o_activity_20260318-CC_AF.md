The next cluster meeting will take place **on April 29, 2026, from 1:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m.** This time, there will be a hands-on session on DANTE led by our chair, Michael Markert from the VZG.

DANTE is a web service for maintaining and publishing vocabularies, provided by the GBV Central Office for NFDI4Objects institutions as well. At our hands-on session, we want to work together to create and edit records in the DANTE test pool so that new users can familiarize themselves with the functionality. 

Feel free to bring sample data related to people, organizations, places, and subject terms to try out!

It would be great if experienced DANTE users could also join us to help answer questions.
