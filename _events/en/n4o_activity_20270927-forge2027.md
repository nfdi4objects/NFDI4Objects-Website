### The conference organised by the [DHd Working Group on ‘Research Data Management in the Humanities’ (gwFDM)](https://digitalhumanities.de/ag-geisteswissenschaftliches-forschungsdatenmanagement-gwfdm/) brings together stakeholders from academia, data infrastructures and research data management who are involved in the generation, use, processing, networking and long-term preservation of research data in the humanities.

**👉 Save the date now: 27–29 September 2027 | Academy of Sciences and Literature, Mainz**

The conference is organised by a team from several institutions ([(Academy of Sciences and Literature | Mainz,](https://www.adwmainz.de/) [University of Trier](https://www.uni-trier.de/), [Mainz University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hs-mainz.de/), [mainzed,](https://mainzed.org/) [Technical University of Darmstadt](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/)) in collaboration with the four NFDI consortia in the humanities and cultural studies within the [Humanities@NFDI](https://www.nfdi.de/humanitiesnfdi/) network (NFDI4Objects, [NFDI4Culture,](https://nfdi4culture.de/de/index.html) [NFDI4Memory](https://4memory.de/) and [Text+](https://text-plus.org/)).

### Further information on the theme, the Call for Papers and the programme will be published on the conference website [https://forge27.de](https://forge27.de).
