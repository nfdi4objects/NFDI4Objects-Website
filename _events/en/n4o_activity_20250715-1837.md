Since 2025, the **Digitalization Working Group of the [Dachverband Archäologischer Studierendenvertretungen e.V. (DASV e.V.)](https://www.dasv-ev.org/)** has been organizing a workshop series on QGIS for archaeologists.

Since the [2nd Workshop](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/events/n4o_activity_20250715-1837/#2nd-qgis-workshop--july-2025), the series has been held in cooperation with **NFDI4Objects**, with **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/) as instructor**.

The slides and datasets from previous workshops have been published on Zenodo and can be accessed via the following link:

**[Link to the workshop slides on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18347010)**

The workshops are recorded. Click on the event you are interested in to access further information and the recording:

**[1st QGIS-Workshop - Introduction in QGIS](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/events/n4o_activity_20250715-1837/#1st-qgis-workshop--january-2025)**

**[2nd QGIS-Workshop - Advanced Introduction in QGIS](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/events/n4o_activity_20250715-1837/#2nd-qgis-workshop--july-2025)**

**[3rd QGIS-Workshop - Coordinate Reference Systems, Map Projections and Local Coordinate Reference Systems in QGIS](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/en/events/n4o_activity_20250715-1837/#3rd-qgis-workshop--january-2026)**

### 4th QGIS Workshop – July 2026

The 4th QGIS Workshop organized by the **Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V.** took place online via Zoom on **July 16, 2026, at 6:00 PM (s.t.)** in cooperation with **[Kai-Christian Bruhn](https://i3mainz.hs-mainz.de/team/kaichristianbruhn/)** from **NFDI4Objects**.

The workshop focused on **digitizing and mapping in QGIS**.

The first virtual QGIS Workshop organized by the Digitalization Working Group of DASV e.V. took place online via Zoom on **January 31, 2025** and was organized by two students.

The workshop covered a **basic introduction to QGIS** as well as the **development of a first small project**.
