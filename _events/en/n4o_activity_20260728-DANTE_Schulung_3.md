**On 28 July 2026, from 1.00 pm to 2.00 pm**, we invite you to a practice-oriented Community Cluster meeting focusing on the DANTE web service, aimed at users with experience of DANTE or complex vocabularies – with CC Chair [Michael Markert](https://www.nfdi4objects.net/persons/michaelmarkert/) (VZG).

💡 DANTE supports the maintenance and publication of controlled vocabularies and is used, amongst other things, in the #NFDI4Objects context.

🛠️ During the hands-on session, the focus will be on your questions, particularly regarding special cases that are difficult to reconcile with the DANTE data model or the import of entire vocabularies.

👉 Please feel free to bring along some sample data (persons, organisations, places, subject terms)!
