#### The NFDI—German National Research Data Infrastructure—consortia [DataPLANT](https://www.nfdi4plants.org/), [FAIRagro](https://fairagro.net/), [NFDI4Biodiversity](https://www.nfdi4biodiversity.org/de/), [NFDI4BIOIMAGE](https://nfdi4bioimage.de/), [NFDI4Microbiota](https://nfdi4microbiota.de/) and NFDI4Objects convened on April 15–16, 2026, at the Julius Kühn Institute in Berlin for the Biodata Interest Group workshop.
  
The workshop served to operationalize the group's [Memorandum of Understanding](https://zenodo.org/records/18694155) by identifying synergies in services, training, and infrastructure.  
Workshop discussions facilitated the exchange of perspectives on a potential strategic roadmap for 2026–2035, the exploration of functional data modalities for cross-consortial integration, and the identification of areas for future coordination to support a more sustainable and collaborative research data infrastructure in the biological sciences.

This report summarizes the workshop’s collaborative mapping, discussion sessions, and the resulting shared interests for the Biodata Interest Group.
