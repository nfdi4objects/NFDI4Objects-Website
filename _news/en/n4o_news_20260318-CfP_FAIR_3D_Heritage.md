We are pleased to share the **call for papers** for the FAIR 3D HERITAGE Conference from our network. The event addresses challenges in the field of **3D heritage**, ranging from viewer infrastructures and documentation standards to the long-term preservation of digital cultural assets. We particularly welcome submissions that combine several of these topics.

The workshop invites the submission of extended abstracts (max. 500 words, British English). The **deadline has been extended to 31 March 2026**; acceptance notifications will be sent on 15 April 2026. Further information and the full Call for Papers can be found on the [conference website](https://ai-mainz.github.io/fair-3d-heritage); submissions can be made via [this form.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScVl1TOcIvWDB_9jx-GIKDGhQjJZTcdNSqiaRhjgbTlbXG0RA/viewform)

All accepted papers will be published in an open-access volume of the Computing in Art and Architecture series. Participation is free of charge.
